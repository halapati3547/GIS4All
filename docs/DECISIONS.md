
## API and UI

- **Data loading.** `api/main.py` loads `data/candidates.json` + `data/meta.json` at startup. If `candidates.json` is missing it falls back to `data/sample/*.sample.json` (made-up sites, top-level `"SAMPLE"` key) and sets `meta.using_sample=true`; the UI then shows a yellow "Showing SAMPLE data" banner. Restart the server after the pipeline writes new data.
- **meta.json shape (assumed).** `{city, built_at, sources:[{id,name,url,checked,status:"ok"|"failed",rows,affects:[test ids]}], weights:{...,label:"ASSUMPTION"}, counts:{...}}`. Any missing key is filled from `candidates.json`. `affects` drives the "goes UNKNOWN if failed" column.
- **Hazard exclusion.** A site counts as excluded if `excluded_by_hazard` is true or `hazards.flood/fire.result == "FAIL"`. `excludeHazardUnknown` drops sites whose flood or fire result is UNKNOWN.
- **Pathway filter.** `pathway=X` keeps sites with a pathway of that id whose status is not FAIL. `pathway=HE` also matches `housing_element.listed`.
- **Pathway ids** expected: `SB9, SB684, AB2011, SB4, SB79, HE`. Statuses: `PASS, FAIL, UNKNOWN, FLAG` (INFO renders grey).
- **Export.** `for_lane_2.pathway_is_ministerial` is true when SB9/SB684/AB2011/SB4 is PASS; `clock_days: 60` is labelled ASSUMPTION.
- **Claude.** Placeholder by default: with `MOCK_CLAUDE` set or no `APP_ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, `/api/explain` returns a template built from pathway statuses and unknowns, tagged `source:"offline template"`. Templates are not cached so a later real call can replace them; real responses are cached to `data/explanations/{apn}.json`. Real path: `claude-sonnet-5-5`, max_tokens 700, 20 s timeout, 1 retry, numbers not in the input replaced with "[see card]".
- **Address lookup** returns 501 `{"detail":"address lookup lands in M6"}`; the UI shows it inline.
- **Front end.** Vanilla JS, Leaflet 1.9.4 from cdnjs, OSM tiles; no build step. Stacks below 1000 px.

## Pipeline

- **Centroids (D1).** The SCAG MapServer ignores `returnCentroid=true` (no centroid in the response), so we fetch `returnGeometry=true&geometryPrecision=5&outSR=4326` and use the mean of the outer-ring vertices. 42,896 rows -> 42,684 unique APNs (stacked/duplicate APNs: keep the largest polygon).
- **LU24 codes (D2).** No coded-value domain and no renderer labels on the layer, but the layer metadata `description` contains the full "2024 SCAG Land Use Codes" legend. This **contradicts the spec's assumed families** (6xxx vacant, 8xxx open space, 3xxx industrial): in SCAG's legend 13xx is industrial, 18xx open space, 1900/3000/3100/3200/3300 vacant, 2xxx agriculture. - **Housing element (D3).** Fields: `Assessor_Parcel_Number`, `Jurisdiction`, `Low_Income_HE_Capacity`, `Moderate_Income_HE_Capacity`, `Above_Moderate_Income_HE_Capacity`, `Total_HE_Capacity`. - **Transit.** D7 243 HQTA polygons, D8 19 stops (805 m haversine).
- **Approximations.** AB 2011 adjacency = industrial-use (LU 13xx) centroid within 60 m; SB 4 buffer = within 366 m; SB 4 perimeter = >=75% of neighbouring centroids within 100 m have urban use (not vacant/open space/agriculture; blank/unknown codes ignored).
- **Result values.** Besides PASS/FAIL/UNKNOWN, tests may be `FLAG` (liquefaction, SB 79) or `N/A` (ab2011.corridor); a pathway with only PASS/FLAG/N/A is PASS. Because `haz.historic`, `sb9.tenancy`, `sb4.owner_proxy` and `haz.wetland_farmland` are always UNKNOWN, **no statutory pathway can reach PASS today**; best_pathway is therefore at most 12, and the max score this run is 52. best_pathway considers SB9/SB684/AB2011/SB4 only (not SB79/HE).

- **Tax-default acquisition lead (D14, D15).** `python -m pipeline.taxdefault` writes `data/tax_default_leads.json`; `build.py` and `lookup.py` only read it. Sources: 2025 list `https://www.octreasurer.gov/sites/ttc/files/2025-06/Impending%20Power%20to%20Sell%20-%20June%208.pdf` (the sibling "Notice of Impending..." file has no parcels), 2024 list `https://www.octreasurer.gov/sites/ttc/files/2024-09/Notice%20of%20Power%20and%20Intent%20to%20Sell%20Tax-Defaulted%20Property%20June%202024.pdf`, cached in `data/raw/ttc/`, parsed with `pdftotext -layout`. The guide's single regex merged side-by-side table columns (one 2025 row swallowed a neighbouring row, including its name, into the location group), so rows are split on their `NO.###` markers instead and the amount is the first `$` figure in the segment: 2025 = 242 rows (95 timeshare, 147 kept), 2024 = 305 rows (134 timeshare, 171 kept), 303 distinct APNs; the guide's counts (234 / 133-151) came from that regex. Two 2024 rows print no amount (`redemption_amount` null). Timeshare books 898/899/916 dropped; condo and mobile-home books kept. No city filter on the notice text: the D1 join restricts to Santa Ana (28 APNs; 502-241-08 is North Tustin and correctly drops). Dates are read from the notice: power to sell since 2025-07-01 / 2024-07-02 (earliest wins), redemption as of 2025-06-30 / 2024-07-01 (newest list wins; the guide said July 1 for 2025, the PDF says June 30).

ASSUMPTION: the flag means delinquent secured taxes on the roll the map was built from, not "subject to power to sell" (a payment plan or a recent redemption can still show 1). Redemption / assessed land value ratio and the assessed land value (Prop 13 floor) are carried as ASSUMPTION signals on `acquisition_lead.signals` and in the Lane 3 export hint.
- **CH8 is informational, not statutory.** `chapter8()` is appended after SB 79 / HE; `STATUTORY` and `best_pathway` are unchanged. Status FLAG with a live lead, FAIL when the county map shows it cleared, UNKNOWN without a lead (absence is not evidence). `ch8.objection_window` and `ch8.board_hearing` are confirm items. CH8 UNKNOWN on a no-lead parcel is left out of `unknowns`.
- **Shared lead bucket.** The 15-point `owner_lead` score bucket now means any acquisition lead (SB 4 proxy or tax-default lead, never 30); key name unchanged. A tax-default lead also keeps an industrial/open-space-zoned parcel in the ranked list. ASSUMPTION: a county-listed tax-defaulted parcel is as strong a lead as a faith-owner proxy. ## Lead (integration)

- **CLEAR status.** The spec made four tests permanently UNKNOWN (tenant occupancy, historic district, wetlands/farmland, owner proxy), so no pathway could ever PASS and every top site scored 52. Tests now carry `kind: data | confirm`. A pathway is FAIL on any FAIL, UNKNOWN if a data test could not run, CLEAR if only confirm items remain, PASS otherwise. CLEAR earns the same 25 best-pathway points as PASS and the card shows "N to confirm".
- **SB 4 gated on the owner lead.** `sb4.owner_proxy` stays a data test. Without a lead SB 4 is UNKNOWN; with an IRS-registry lead on the parcel it is PASS (labelled PROXY) and SB 4 can reach CLEAR. Otherwise nearly every parcel would show "SB 4 CLEAR", which is misleading.
- **Owner leads wired in.** `data/owner_leads.json` (203 parcels) feeds the facts, the score (+15), the exclusion rule (an industrial or open-space zoned parcel stays in if it has a lead) and who_to_call (the organisation first, then the city).
- **Live lookups** are registered in the API's by-APN map so Explain and Export work for an unranked address.
- **Addresses** fetched for the top 1,000 (748 have one; vacant lots often have none).

- **Fire (D5), 2026-11-07 13:40.** The OC LRA fire layer returns 0 polygons for the Santa Ana bbox. Upstream made that UNKNOWN, which blocked every pathway from CLEAR. Checked: the layer answers, holds 21 Very High zones countywide, nearest about 10 km from Santa Ana. `polys(..., empty_unknown=True)` now verifies an empty city result with a countywide `returnCountOnly` call (cached at `data/raw/fire_lra_count/`): count > 0 means PASS, a failed or zero count keeps UNKNOWN. The layer predates CAL FIRE's March 2025 LRA maps (new zones in Buena Park, Costa Mesa, Huntington Beach, Laguna Hills, Placentia), so a human-confirm item `haz.fire_2025` sits in every pathway, citing the county's 2025 FHSZ page. No machine-readable 2025 LRA layer for Orange County was found; the hosted FHSZ_2025 copy on services3.arcgis.com covers Northern California only, and osfm.fire.ca.gov blocks scripted access.
